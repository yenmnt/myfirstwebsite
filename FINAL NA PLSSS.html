A Little About Me

Yen

Hi, I’m Mariel Jane Balbino Mante, but people call me Yen! I’m 22, proudly from Malapatan, Sarangani Province, and currently a nursing student.

I’m an optimistic, outgoing, and compassionate person who believes deeply in the power of love: love for my family, love for my friends, and the kind of love that shows up as genuine care for other people. I’m a proud family girl and a lover girl at heart, and I think both have shaped the way I see the world: with softness, gratitude, and a willingness to care deeply.

Nursing gave me more than a profession to look forward to. It gave me a sense of purpose. Somewhere along the way, I found three words that continue to guide me: to serve, love, and care. They remind me that caring for people is not only about knowing what to do, but also about being present, compassionate, and human.

Outside nursing, I’m happiest when I’m surrounded by the people I love, playing a good game of pickleball, going on spontaneous trips, or simply making little memories that become stories worth keeping. I love a last-minute adventure, but I’ll admit that I’m happiest when there’s a plan to hold onto. I’m also unapologetically a heavy packer. I like being prepared for every possible “what if?” because you never know what you might need. And yes, a bottle of alcohol is basically a permanent resident in my bag.

At my core, I’m someone who finds joy in people, purpose in nursing, comfort in family, and beauty in the little things. I’m still learning, growing, and figuring life out one experience at a time, but I hope to always remain someone who serves with purpose, loves wholeheartedly, and cares genuinely.

The Roots I Carry

I am proud to be full-blooded Blaan and part of an Indigenous Peoples community. Being Blaan is more than a part of my identity; it is woven into my story, my roots, and the person I am becoming. It connects me to a culture, a community, and a heritage that I carry with pride wherever I go.

I carry where I come from with me, not as something that holds me back, but as something that grounds me. As I continue to grow, meet new people, and build my own path, I hope to always remember, honor, and carry with pride the roots that shaped me.

Wherever life takes me, I will always carry my roots with pride.

my nursing journey

Learning, Growing, Becoming.

Nursing was never just about learning how to care for the sick. It has become a journey of discovering the kind of person I want to be: someone who serves with purpose, loves with compassion, and cares with intention. Through nursing school, I have learned that caring for another person requires more than knowledge and skills. It requires patience, presence, empathy, courage, and the willingness to keep learning. Every duty, patient encounter, challenge, and small victory has shaped me a little more. Nursing has taught me that I am capable of more than I once thought, and that sometimes, simply being present can already mean so much to someone.

Lessons Learned
  • Nursing has taught me that competence and compassion should always go hand in hand.
  • I have learned to trust myself more. Things that once made me nervous have gradually become experiences I can approach with greater confidence.
  • I have learned that growth does not always feel like growth while it is happening. Sometimes, you only realize how far you've come when someone else notices the person you've become.
  • I have learned that being a nurse is not about knowing everything. It is about being willing to learn, ask questions, remain humble, and always strive to do better for the people entrusted to your care.
  • Most importantly, nursing continues to remind me of the words I want to carry into the profession:

    to serve, love, and care.
